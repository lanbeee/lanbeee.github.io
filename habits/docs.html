The Tings guide

Tings is a local-first planner for habits, tasks, and a week agenda that fits around your real life. This guide covers every feature, screen, and setting — start at Getting started if you're new.

Get started in 2 minutes How the planner thinks Open the app

Use the search box above or the contents on the left to jump around. Throughout the guide you'll see screenshot placeholders marking spots where a real picture of the screen will go.

One-line summary: Tings turns "I want to do X about Y times a week" into a today list and a packed week — without the anxiety of rigid due dates, and without the vagueness of endless to-do lists.

What is Tings?

Tings is a personal planner that lives entirely on your device. It blends three things that usually live in separate apps:

Habit tracker To-do list Week agenda / calendar

Each thing you want to track is a Ting. A Ting can be a habit (repeats on a rhythm), a task (a one-off job, optionally with a due date), or a fixed-time event (a hard appointment like "Dentist Tue 3pm"). All three share one list, one history, and one search.

The planner looks at everything together — your habits, your tasks, the time you actually have free this week, the places you'll be — and produces a short today list plus a week agenda that slots work into the gaps that genuinely exist.

Screenshot: the home list — today's agenda plus your habits and tasks

Who it's for

  • People who want to build habits (exercise, reading, prayer, deep work) but hate rigid streaks.
  • People juggling recurring work and one-off tasks who don't want two apps.
  • Anyone who wants their planner to respect real life — sleep, meals, commuting, the gym being across town.
  • Privacy-conscious users: no account, no server, no sync. Your data never leaves the device.

How it thinks

A few ideas shape every screen. Understanding them once makes the whole app click.

Rhythm over dates

You say "about 3× in 7 days," not "every Monday." Tings tracks whether you're on pace, not whether you broke a streak.

No streak guilt

Missing a day resets nothing. A behind card just means "do it soon" — the target rolls forward.

Capacity is real

The week has finite free hours. Tings knows your sleep, work, meals and travel, so the agenda only plans into time that actually exists.

Whole week at once

Tings plans the whole week together, not day-by-day. Something that doesn't fit today gets pulled forward to tomorrow, not dropped.

Local & private

Everything is stored on your device. Export a backup when you want; there is no cloud to leak.

One pipeline

Habits, tasks, and events share one list with common search, topics, places, and history — not three separate silos.

Getting started

From a blank install to a working today list in a couple of minutes.

  1. Open the app. Tap the Tings logo (top-left) any time for help and settings. A brand-new install shows an empty home with a hint.

  2. Try samples (optional but recommended). Tap the empty message, or open the Tings menu → sample habits. Add one demo or a whole tagged set — timers, places, breakable tasks, prayer demos and more are in there. Sample items are tagged so you can remove them in one tap later. (See Sample habits.)

    Screenshot: the sample habits sheet

  3. Add your first Ting. Tap the + button (bottom nav or top bar). Name it, pick habit or task, set "how often," and tap add. The detail view opens so you can fine-tune. See The add sheet.

  4. Log it. On the home list, tap the pulsing icon (or double-tap the card) to log a completion. That's the core loop.

  5. Tell Tings about your week. When you're ready, open settings and set your busy times (sleep, work, meals) and places. The agenda immediately becomes smarter.

You can use Tings forever without configuring busy times or places — it still tracks habits and tasks perfectly. Those settings only make the planner sharper.

How the planner works

The planner is what turns "I want to read 3× a week and exercise daily and call mom and do a 6h deep-work block" into a sane day. It's worth understanding the big picture before the details.

The short version

Each time you open the app (or change something), Tings builds the whole week at once in a background worker, then shows you today's slice. It places things in this strict priority order — top tier always beats the tier below:

  1. Hard rules win. Capacity, busy blocks, allowed time windows, pinned items — never broken.
  2. Maximise hours placed. Fill as many real minutes as possible across the week. If a can-wait item is squatting on a slot a daily block needs, it's peeled to another day.
  3. Minimise travel. Co-located errands share one trip; far clusters get grouped to cut commuting.
  4. Sooner & higher priority. When hours and travel are unaffected, do things sooner and favour higher priority.
  5. Preferences. Preferred time/place/weekday are soft nudges — they never beat hours, travel, or urgency.

The inputs

Busy times

Recurring blocks to avoid — sleep, work, meals, commute. See Capacity & open hours.

Open hours

The budget left after busy times. This caps how many minutes of items a day can hold, not just clock time.

Fixed events

Timed tasks and appointments — placed by clock first, everything else works around them.

Habit fields

Duration, priority, flexibility, allowed/preferred windows, breakable + min chunk, places, days.

Places & travel

Your saved places plus travel edges between them (drive/walk/bike/transit).

Order links

"Before/after" chains you set up — temporary for today, or recurring in Schedule. See Habit order.

Two engines: simple vs smarter packing

There are two packers, switched by the smarter packing toggle (Settings → advanced; on by default):

  • Off (simple) — a fast greedy pass. Instant; used for previews and as a fallback.
  • On (smarter) — GLPK chooses the best non-overlapping set from generated time-and-location options for each day, then a complete-day route optimizer minimizes travel across every selected item and fixed location anchor. Breakable work fills the remaining gaps, and a week-level repair pass moves deferrable work to improve coverage.

Both satisfy the same hard rules. If GLPK cannot load or a day's solve reaches its background budget, Tings falls back to the simple packer so the agenda still renders. While GLPK is running you may briefly see a "fast preview" state in the day audit.

What happens when a day overflows

When capacity runs out, the weight function picks winners: a scarce narrow window beats a broad higher-priority item (the gym opens 6–7am; reading can happen anytime). Within the same scarcity band, higher priority wins (P0 before P5), and within a priority your home creation order is kept. Anything that can't fit today is pulled forward to another eligible day rather than dropped — only items with no viable day anywhere are left out. A habit whose hard window is genuinely too small for its duration is never force-fit.

The planner evaluates eligible days and repairs the week after daily packing, so an item that does not fit today can quietly land tomorrow or later — you do not have to reschedule it by hand.

Planner scenarios

The best way to understand the planner is to see what it does in real situations. Each scenario shows a setup and the resulting agenda.

1. Packed day drops the lowest priority
You set up
  • Cardio, 50 min, P0
  • Reading, 50 min, P5
  • Only 60 min of capacity today
The agenda
  • Cardio gets the slot
  • Reading is dropped today, rolled forward to a lighter day
  • The unused gap is flagged "budget-capped," not a scheduler miss
2. Errands across town share one trip
You set up
  • A morning home routine
  • Two errands at far-but-adjacent places (~28 km away, ~500 m apart)
  • Travel mode: walking
The agenda
  • Both errands land on the same day with a short hop between them
  • A thin travel card reads "leave by … · Nm · Home → FarA"
  • Total commuting is a fraction of two separate round-trips
3. A breakable task splits into chunks
You set up
  • "Write report," 90 min, breakable, min chunk 30
  • Day fragmented by blocks into a 40-min gap and a 50-min gap
The agenda
  • Splits into 40 + 50 (the largest valid piece per gap — never 30+30+30)
  • If forced into three 30-min gaps plus a 10-min gap, you get 30+30+30+10
  • The first card shows a progress crown; later chunks show trail dots
4. Habit chained after a morning routine
You set up
  • Three habits; you drag one to be right after "Morning routine"
  • An unrelated high-priority item also wants that slot
The agenda
  • The chained habit sits directly after the routine, nothing between them
  • The unrelated item is the one removed, not the linked pair
  • Order-link marks on the card show the partner's emoji
5. Prayer-anchored window beats a flexible P0
You set up
  • "Sunrise stretch," 5 min, allowed sunrise+5 → sunrise+35, P2
  • "Deep work," 60 min, anywhere, P0
  • Sleep until sunrise−8h
The agenda
  • The scarce sunrise window wins the morning gap for stretch
  • The flexible P0 deep work places outside that gap despite higher priority
  • A prayer whose window already closed rolls to tomorrow's window
6. Sleep block shifts with the sunrise
You set up
  • The sample sun-based sleep block (sunrise−8h → sunrise)
  • Your city is set
The agenda
  • Sleep always totals exactly 8h, wrapping overnight across seasons
  • The first open minute tracks sunrise — earlier in summer, later in winter
  • A sunrise habit between sleep and breakfast now places correctly
7. Plan-by defers to a lighter day
You set up
  • Work, 6h breakable, P0
  • A 1h clinic meeting tomorrow
  • Two plan-by errands (2h each) due later this week
The agenda
  • Work still hits 6h tomorrow
  • At most one errand lands tomorrow; both place on quieter days within their plan-by windows
  • If they'd fit the slack, both place ASAP — no over-deferring
8. "Do this now" jumps something to the top
You set up
  • Three equal-priority tasks on today
  • You long-press-drag one to the very top of today
The agenda
  • That task is pinned to the top and starts a session timer from now
  • It auto-logs after its duration, then clears
  • Completing any habit clears today's order edges touching it
9. Do-early pulls a flexible item into today
You set up
  • "Laundry," target 2× / 7d, flexibility 2 days
  • Its target day is overloaded; today has 4h free
The agenda
  • Laundry is pulled into today, shown under today with an early pill
  • If today were full, it'd fall back to "coming up" with no early pill
10. Open-time pill reveals the gaps
You set up
  • Busy blocks for sleep, work, dinner
  • A few habits with windows
The agenda
  • Each day header shows an "open time" pill: "3h open · 1h biggest stretch"
  • Tap it for a free/busy strip and a list of every open gap
  • No pill when the day has under 10 min free
11. Preferred time is a soft nudge
You set up
  • A habit with an allowed window all day and a preferred start of 2pm
The agenda
  • Placed at ~2pm when the session fits
  • But the nudge never overrides a hard allowed window or a busy block
  • If 2pm is blocked, it falls back to the clock

Capacity & open hours

The planner treats your week as a finite container of open minutes. Two things define it:

Busy times

Recurring blocks you don't want filled — sleep, work, meals, commute, school. Add them in Settings → busy times. Each has a start/end (fixed clock or dynamic), chosen weekdays, and optional place. You can override a single date without touching the recurring rule.

Open hours

What's left after busy times. The budget caps minutes of doable items per day, not raw clock time — so a 600-min budget means 600 min of items even if the clock has more literal gaps.

How free time shows up

  • Open-time pill on each day header — tap for a free/busy strip and the list of gaps.
  • Day audit — triple-tap a day header for a detailed scorecard (capacity, eligible work, the planner's decision trace). Power-user tool.
  • Cancelling a busy block for a date frees its exact minutes and writes a one-day override; undo restores it.

If a day is overfull, the lowest-priority items are dropped first and rolled forward — Tings never silently overbooks you.

Priority & urgency

Every Ting has a priority from P0 (critical) to P5 (someday). Priority claims today's time first, and is dropped first when a day overflows.

PriorityMeaningUse for
P0Critical — claims time firstNon-negotiables, deadlines
P1HighImportant recurring work
P2Normal (default)Most habits
P3LowNice-to-haves
P4LowerBackground goals
P5Someday — dropped firstBacklog, wishful items

Urgency is separate from priority. A low-priority habit that's been missed for days still surfaces, while a high-priority one that's comfortably ahead stays calm. The card's status color reflects the combination. Note: a scarce narrow time window beats a broad higher-priority item — the planner protects things that can only happen in one slot.

Priority also shows as a colored accent on the card left bar (P0 = strongest tone, P5 = muted).

Rhythm & flexibility

Two numbers define a habit's rhythm:

Target

times × in N days — e.g. 3× in 7d or 1× in 30d. The crown dial in the add/detail sheet picks the period (1–183 days).

Flexibility

How many days early a session can count. "2× in 7d, 2d flexible" lets Tuesday's session move to Sunday if the week is packed. On tasks, flexibility flips: how many days before the due date it starts surfacing.

Based on these, a card shows a status instead of a brittle streak (see Status). There's no streak counter and nothing "resets" — the rhythm just rolls forward.

Status: done / almost / behind

Every card carries a status driven by how recent your last log is versus your target rhythm:

done — on pace almost — close behind — needs attention new — not started yet
  • Build habits: "done" when last log is within target days; "almost" up to ~1.35× target; "behind" beyond that.
  • Limit habits: direction inverts — "done" when enough days have passed since the last log.
  • Stop habits: tracked by clean days; each clean day is a green dot.
  • New: never logged yet — surfaced gently so it doesn't nag.

The status pill on a card can show a percentage too (e.g. 72%), tinted to match. Toggle which marks appear in Look & feel.

Tings, explained

Every item in your list is a Ting. There are three families, each modelling a different kind of commitment.

Habits — build, limit, stop

Habits repeat on a rhythm. When you add one you choose a kind:

build — keep it up limit — do less stop — quit entirely
build

"Exercise 3× in 7 days." You want this to happen often. Turns green when you're on pace.

limit

"Social media under 30 min/day." You're capping something. Logging still counts; the target frames it as a ceiling.

stop

"No snooze." A quit. Clean days (no slip) are good days; the month grid shows your streak of clean days.

Each habit has a target ("how often") like 2× in 7d, optional flexibility, a duration, and a priority. Stop habits deprioritise in the today list (they're absence-tracking, not something to schedule).

Tasks — one-off

A task happens once. No rhythm — just an optional due date. "Call the dentist back." Completion is simply your first log.

  • No due date → a "someday" task; sits in coming up until you plan or finish it.
  • Due date only → surfaces as it approaches (controlled by its flexibility window). A flexibility of 0 makes it a firm deadline.
  • Due date + time → becomes a fixed appointment on the agenda, exported to your phone calendar as a timed event.

Fixed-time events

An event is a hard point in time — "Dentist Tue 3:00pm," "Flight 6am." Events are never rescored or shuffled; they're placed by clock and everything else is planned around them. They appear on the agenda and can be exported to your phone calendar. (You create one by giving a task a date and a time, or via calendar import.)

Emoji & visual identity

Tings leans on emoji to make your list scannable at a glance. A coloured circle with a familiar glyph is far faster to recognise than a wall of text — that's why the pulse icon on every card, the detail header, and the order-link marks all show the habit's emoji.

Auto-suggested from the name

When you type a name, Tings suggests an emoji after a short pause. The matcher tries, in order:

  1. Multi-word phrases — "walk the dog" → 🐕 (longest phrase wins).
  2. Single strong keywords — "run" → 🏃.
  3. Fuzzy matches — small typos still resolve.
  4. Modifier words (morning, daily, weekly) as a last resort.

The map covers ~300 concepts — fitness, food, faith (pray, quran, dua, ramadan, masjid…), study, work, parenting, home, sports, money, travel, screen time, and abstract goals (resolution → ⭐, quit/stop → 🚫, limit → ⛔).

Quick pick & your own

No good match? Tap the emoji preview tile to open the editor and either type any emoji or pick from 18 neutral quick-pick chips (🔥 🌟 📌 🏆 ✨ …). These are deliberately chosen so a chip never fights an auto-suggestion.

Emoji background colours (disambiguation)

Every emoji sits on a coloured disk from a 12-colour palette (teal, amber, red, purple, blue, green, pink, orange, indigo, cyan, lime, slate) — or no background. Tings auto-disambiguates: if two habits share the same emoji, the second one is auto-assigned a different colour so the emoji + colour pair stays unique across your list.

🏃 teal🏃 amber🏃 purple no background

Default icon when blank

Leave the emoji blank and the card shows a tasteful default icon for the type (a heart-ish glyph for build, down-arrow for limit, etc.) in a muted tone.

Once you manually edit an emoji, auto-suggestion stops overriding it — your choice sticks. In the detail sheet, a habit that already had an emoji when you opened it is never auto-changed on rename.

Home: today's agenda + your list

Home is the screen you'll see most. It's today's agenda plus your full list of habits and tasks — not a dump of everything, but not just today either. What floats up: pinned items, things due or ready today, fixed appointments, and anything you've planned for soon. Everything else sits in coming up / the rest.

Regions, top to bottom

Top bar

Tings logo (tap for help & settings — "how it works"), search field, calendar icon, +, search toggle. Hides on scroll-down, reappears on scroll-up.

Quota bar

Only shows if storage gets large — warns that old dense activity may be compacted.

Filter row

Presence picker, place chips, topic chips. Narrows the list without changing what's due. Hidden in minimal mode.

The list

Day section headers + habit/task cards + busy-block/travel cards. A loading skeleton shows on cold open until the planner supplies the week.

Bottom nav

Calendar · + (emphasised centre) · search. Search and calendar are hidden until you have enough to use them.

Reading a card

A card packs a lot into a small space. Every mark is optional (toggle in Look & feel).

Pulse icon (left)

The coloured circle with your emoji. Colour reflects status: teal (on track/fresh), amber (due soon), red (overdue/urgent), purple (a quit habit, recent), quiet (a never-logged task). Tap to log.

Name + agenda pill

The title (ellipsises first), and if it has a placed agenda row, a small time pill like "5:30p".

Status line (cue)

A short phrase: "Ready to start", "Planned today", "Clean today", "N days clean", "Snoozed for now", "Needs a day by today", "2d overdue", "Scheduled", "Someday"…

Context marks

Small icon pills: progress %, early, doing-now, order-link (arrow + partner emoji), sample, pin, due/scheduled/plan-by, rhythm, duration, flexibility, day-schedule, time-window, topics, place, next-plan, snoozed-until.

Trail / crown

Most cards show a two-week trail of dots (one for last week, seven for this week, today highlighted). Breakable cards show a progress crown slider instead. A running session timer overlays a progress bar.

Quick actions (right)

Three icon buttons on full cards: activity, snooze, remove (mirror the swipe actions). Hidden in minimal mode and on breakable cards.

Logging & value logs

Three ways to log

  • Tap the pulse icon — quick log right now.
  • Double-tap the card body — same quick log (a single tap on the body opens detail instead).
  • Detail header pulse — logs from inside the detail view.

Taps are forgiving: if your finger drifts while scrolling, the tap is suppressed so you never log by accident.

Breakable cards log a chunk, not the whole budget

Tapping the pulse on a breakable habit logs a suggested chunk (the min, or the placed partial) — never the full remaining budget — so one tap can't accidentally "finish" a multi-hour task. Drag the crown to set a larger chunk; the pulse commits it.

Logging a value or note

If "log a value or note" is on (Effort page), each log can carry a number (weight, reps, pages, $) and an optional note. A small sheet asks for it; tap skip to log without a value.

Plans vs actual logs

You can also plan a session for a future/past day from the activity sheet or month grid. Plans are tentative; an actual log later consumes the plan. Toasts offer quick follow-ups like "done now", "plan instead", or "plan today" (see Toasts).

Swipe actions

Swipe a card horizontally to reveal quick actions. Swipe right reveals the left shelf; swipe left reveals the right shelf. Only one row is open at a time.

Swipe right → (left shelf)

pin / unpin keep (samples only) activity session / stop
  • Pin — keep above the auto-ordering (e.g. a morning anchor).
  • Keep — sample habits only: converts it into a permanent habit.
  • Activity — opens the per-habit activity and trend sheet.
  • Session / stop — start or stop a focus timer (timer-eligible habits only).

Swipe left → (right shelf)

snooze remove
  • Snooze — open the snooze sheet: 1d / 3d / 7d / 14d, or 1 / 2 repetitions.
  • Remove — delete, with an undo in the toast right after.

The crown-dial area is excluded from swipe arming, so turning a breakable dial never swipes the card.

Reorder & doing now

Placed agenda rows can be reordered by hand for today. This is separate from the recurring Schedule links.

Long-press, then drag

  1. Long-press a card for a moment — it vibrates, a six-dot grip appears, and the card is armed.

  2. Drag it vertically. Drop indicator lines appear between rows.

  3. Drop between two cards to set an adjacency, or at the very top of today for "doing now".

The "Reorder?" sheet — temporary links

Dropping between cards opens "Reorder? · Just for today. Clears when done." For each neighbour you pick:

off

No link.

below / above

"Sometime" adjacency — comes after/before the neighbour, but other things can go between.

right below / right above

"Direct" adjacency — nothing may go between them.

These order links are today-only. Completing a habit clears today's edges touching it; completing a one-shot task also clears future-day edges. The Actions page lists every link with an unlink button and a clear-all.

"Do this now" — drop at the top of today

Dropping a card at the very top of today opens "Do this Now? · Keeps this on top and logs it automatically after Nm." Confirming:

  • Pins it to the top of today from this moment.
  • Starts a session timer that auto-logs the habit when its target time passes, then clears.
  • Optionally links the card below (right-above / sometime).

Doing-now is per calendar day and clears on day rollover. Manual mode (if you start a session by hand) never auto-expires — it runs until you stop it.

Order links never conflict with recurring Schedule links — if you try to set one that clashes, Tings tells you to edit Habit order in Schedule instead.

Easier reach

On big phones the top cards can be a stretch. Turn on easier reach (default on) and pull down and hold at the top — the first cards slide down into thumb range for a few seconds. A hard upward flick at the top triggers it too. The gesture auto-dismisses and any touch cancels the hold.

Search & filters

Search

Tap the search icon to look up habits by name, topic, or place. Search is a lookup, not an agenda view — it skips day sections, busy/travel cards, and today/overdue grouping; results are matching habits ranked by relevance. (Search appears once you have ~10 habits or a completed task.)

Filter chips

The row above the list narrows what's shown without changing what's due:

Presence

"Where are you?" — sets today's starting place for travel/leave-by math. Shows "at {place}" / "near {place}" when GPS-matched. Doesn't filter the list.

Place chips

One per place actually used by a habit, plus "anywhere" / "no place". Toggle on/off.

Topic chips

One per topic in use, plus "no topic". Toggle on/off.

Stale filters reset to "all" if the underlying dimension disappears, and sideways scrolling on the chip rows won't accidentally toggle a chip.

Day sections & week view

Home groups your list into day sections. Two layouts, switched by the week by day setting:

  • Week by day (default) — sections for today, tomorrow, then weekday+date ("Wed 15"), followed by overdue, coming up, the rest.
  • Today / overdue / coming up — the classic three buckets plus the rest.

Pinned habits always render first under their own header, regardless of mode. Section headers are sticky and stack as you scroll.

Busy blocks & travel cards

Inside the day sequence you'll also see:

  • Busy cards — "{label} · 9AM–5PM · {place}" with an X to clear it for today. Tap to edit. Consecutive blocks collapse into a merge with a chevron.
  • Travel cards — "leave by {time} · Nm · {from} → {to}" with a pencil if you manually edited it. Tap to edit the leg (or open in maps, or reset to estimate).

The busy blocks & travel setting chooses whether to show them for all days or just the next 12 hours.

Pills & indicators

Open-time pill

On day headers: "3h open · 1h biggest stretch". Tap for the free/busy strip + gap list. Hidden under 10 min free.

Missed / slipped pill

On the today header: "N missed" — habits that were suggested but slipped off today. Tap for the missed sheet (today's misses + "still open from yesterday").

Early pill

On cards pulled forward into today: "early" with a reason in the tooltip ("preferred day is packed", "after {linked}").

Doing-now pill

"auto · Nm" or "session · Nm" on the card being focused right now.

Status pill

"72%" tinted to done/almost/behind, or "new".

Toasts & undo

After most actions a small toast appears with quick follow-ups. The action toast can carry up to four buttons:

undo / restore / show

Always reversible — reverses logging, planning, deleting, snoozing, or freeing a busy block.

open

Re-opens the detail for the item you just logged.

action

Context-sensitive: "done now", "plan instead", "plan today", or "keep plan" after a log.

snooze until planned

After planning a future session: hides the habit until exactly that planned time.

Empty states

Home's empty state changes with context:

  • Brand new: "habits, tasks, and planning" — tap to try samples.
  • No search matches: "no matches — try a habit name, topic, or place".
  • Filter excludes everything: "no habits in {filter}" — tap to reset.
  • All snoozed & hidden: "hidden for now — tap to show".
  • Everything done: "all clear".
  • Nothing active: "nothing active — use Calendar for scheduled items, or + to add".

The add sheet

Tap + to add a Ting. The basics are on top; tap more options for the rest.

Top-level fields

Name

Up to 60 chars. An emoji is auto-suggested from the name; tap the preview tile to edit it (input, quick-pick chips, background colours). See Emoji.

Type

habit or task.

How often

For habits: a crown dial plus times × in N days (1–183). For tasks: a due date field instead. Help text adapts to the kind (build / limit / stop / task).

Due date & time

Tasks only. Date alone = soft deadline (flexibility 0 makes it firm). Date + time = a fixed appointment on the agenda and calendar export.

More options (disclosure)

Priority

P0–P5. "P0 claims today's time first; P5 is dropped first when the day overflows."

Auto mark done

Blank = you tap manually. Set minutes and a planned/scheduled occurrence logs itself that long after its start. Manual sessions keep counting until you stop them.

Topics & places

Tag the item and/or restrict it to places. Tap + new place to drop a pin on the map; tap + new topic to make a label. Place chips cycle preference: off → on → little ☆ → high ★ → avoid –.

On save (limit 300 habits), the sheet closes, the list re-renders, and the new item's detail opens on the Schedule page so you can fine-tune.

Set your usual new-habit defaults once (duration, priority, topics, splitting) and every new Ting starts that way.

The detail view

Tap a card's body (not its icon) to open detail. It's a swipeable pager of six pages with dots at the bottom; on wider screens the same pages behave like tabs. The header card shows the pulse (tap to log), name, and a cue line.

calendar insight schedule effort identity actions

The page you land on is chosen for you: a habit with order constraints opens on actions; a task opens on effort; otherwise a habit opens on calendar. In minimal mode, insight & effort pages are hidden and the essentials (duration, auto-mark, due) move onto the schedule page.

Screenshot: detail view with the six page dots

Month page

A calendar of this habit's history, with prev/next month nav and a label like "Aug 2026 · 5".

  • Summary stats — days, entries, planned counts.
  • Month grid — each day color-coded with a dot: done almost behind planned. Tap a day to open the day-logs sheet for that day/habit.
  • Plan-by date (habits only) — a soft one-off target. "Set a one-off plan-by date to pull this habit into the week planner without picking a specific day." Buttons: this week (sets end of week) and clear. The planner finds a free day on or before it; logging clears it.

Plan-by overrides weekday exclusion — a plan-by-today habit stays on today's agenda even if its allowed weekdays would normally skip today.

Insight page

  • About text — a plain sentence: "Last entry was 2d ago. 5 days left in this rhythm." / "This is 3 days overdue." / "A someday task — pin it or add a due date to bring it forward."
  • Trend label — on pace / behind / overdue / due today / watch / completed / someday / no entries yet.
  • Stats — a score ring (0–100) with a title, plus tiles: since last / until next, usual gap, last-30d done, streak (or clear days for limit/stop), recent gaps pace strip, total entries.
  • Gap-history graph — bar chart of recent gaps with the target line. "Shorter is better" for build; "longer is better" for limit/stop.

Schedule page

Tells the planner when a Ting is allowed and when it's best. Two layers: allowed (hard) and preferred (soft). Toggle between them at the top.

  • Rhythm (habits) — the crown dial + times × in N days.
  • Habit order — persistent schedule links (before/after another habit, direct or sometime, optional same-day).
  • Days — weekday chips (sun–sat) and a "month days" disclosure for specific days of the month (1–31), summarised as "1–5, 15, 22–31".
  • Time window (allowed) / best time (preferred) — see Days & time windows and Dynamic times.

Effort page

Duration

Length of one session (default 30 min). Drives packing.

Flexibility

Days early it can be done (habits) / days before due it surfaces (tasks). "Adds a buffer to your target for planning purposes."

Breakable into chunks

Allow splitting, with a min chunk floor. "Smallest piece when work must be split. Prefer one continuous session when time allows — this is a floor, not a suggested chunk size."

Log a value or note

Track a number each log (weight, reps, pages) plus an optional note.

Auto mark done

Blank = manual tap. For breakables the label becomes "auto-log agenda chunks" — each placed chunk is credited after it ends. "This setting never completes a manual session."

Timer / session

Start a focused session with an optional session target (blank uses duration). Manual sessions count until you stop them.

Due

Tasks only — date and optional time (time makes it a fixed appointment).

Identity page

  • Name + emoji — same editor as the add sheet.
  • Type — habit / task.
  • Kind (habits only) — build / limit / stop.
  • Priority — P0–P5.
  • Topics & places — same chip rows as the add sheet, including + new place.

  • Per-day reorder edges — grouped by day, each "{day} · next" (direct) or "{day} · later", with unlink.

Days & time windows

Days

Pick weekdays (sun–sat) and, behind the "month days" disclosure, specific days of the month (1–31). "Any" is the default.

Time windows — allowed vs preferred

Allowed (hard)

A starts–ends window the planner must respect. Once it closes, the habit leaves today's agenda and is marked overdue.

Preferred (soft)

A "best time" nudge. The day stays open and the habit still places late if needed — the nudge never overrides a busy block.

Two modes per endpoint

  • Fixed — a clock time like 06:00.
  • Dynamic — relative to an anchor: sunrise/sunset/prayer times, or even another habit's last log. Add a signed offset, combine two expressions, and see a live resolved preview.

Each endpoint has a gear toggle to switch between fixed and dynamic, and a clear button. A live "resolved" line shows the current clock time the expression computes to.

Dynamic & prayer-anchored times

Dynamic windows are how Tings handles times that move — sunrise, sunset, prayer times, or "after my workout habit." Any start/end on a habit window or a busy time can be dynamic.

Anchors

sunrisesunset / maghrib fajr · dhuhr · asr · isha another habit clock time

Generic names are Dawn / Noon / Afternoon / Sunset / Night; turn on Islamic prayer names for Fajr / Dhuhr / Asr / Maghrib / Isha.

Building an expression

  1. Pick an anchor (prayer, sunrise/sunset, or a habit).

  2. Add an offset in minutes with a +/− sign toggle (e.g. "sunrise +30", "isha −15m", "sunrise −8h"). Clamped to ±720 min.

  3. Optionally toggle +1d to use next day's anchor (so "sunrise −8h +1d" means tonight's bedtime against tomorrow's sunrise).

  4. Optionally combine a second expression with later of / earlier of — e.g. "earlier of maghrib+1h · isha−15m". The second expression can be another anchor or a clock time.

A live resolved preview shows the clock time, or tells you what's missing ("add a location", "pick a habit", "never logged").

Habit-relative anchors

An anchor can be another habit's last log — "start 15 min after Gym ends." The window updates as that habit gets logged. Cycles (A after B after A) are detected and blocked on save. (Busy times support prayer/fixed anchors only, not habit anchors.)

Prayer anchors need a city or place with coordinates. "Set your city to use prayer times" if you haven't.

Habit order & links

There are two flavours of "this goes with that" — temporary and recurring.

Temporary order links (today only)

Created by dragging cards on home. "Just for today. Clears when done." Sometime or direct adjacency; cleared when the involved habits are completed.

Recurring schedule links

Defined in the Schedule page's habit order block ("add link"). Each link:

Direction

before / after the chosen partner habit.

Adjacency

sometime (other things can go between) or right before/after (direct — nothing between; travel and fixed events can still appear).

Same-day

"Only on days with {partner}" — when on, this habit only plans with the partner (uses flexibility to join early, shown with the early indicator). Multiple same-day links are OR'd — it plans with whichever partner lands that day.

"Link this habit before or after any number of others. Links are OR'd: plan with whichever partner lands that day. 'Right before/after' keeps other movable cards out of the middle; required travel and fixed events can still sit between their times. Same-day pull uses flexibility so this habit can join a partner early (shown with the early indicator)."

Places & travel

A place is a real location with coordinates: Home, Office, Gym. Habits can be restricted to places, and the planner accounts for travel time between consecutive places on the agenda.

Adding places (map picker)

In Settings → locations, tap add place. Search an address, drag the map, drop the pin, or enter coordinates. Each place has a name, address, coordinates, a radius ("how close means you're here", default 75 m), and optional emoji.

Per-place hours

Each place can have open hours and closed days — layered resolution: per-weekday override → closed days → default hours → 24h. A habit's effective window is the intersection of its own window and the place's resolved hours.

Live location (opt-in, private)

Enable live location so Tings knows which place you're currently at. Raw coordinates are never stored or sent — only the matched place id is kept. First enable shows a rationale sheet and requires a tap (an OS requirement).

City

A general area used for sunrise/sunset and prayer calculations when live location is off or a habit has no place. Set automatically from your first place, or type one (e.g. "Lahore"). Required for prayer-anchored windows and the sample sleep block.

Travel time

Travel between places is estimated (drive/walk/bike/transit), cached so it's never recomputed, and shown as a thin card between agenda items. Tap a travel card to override the minutes, open it in maps, or reset to the estimate. The default travel mode is set in locations settings.

Presence — "where are you?"

The presence picker sets today's starting place for travel math (it does not filter the list). Resolution: your manual pick → GPS geofence match → last known place → nearest place.

Home ranking stays location-blind — places only change the agenda order and insert travel cards, not what's "most important." A place used by any habit can't be removed until you clear the reference.

Calendar overview

Tap the calendar icon to open the overview — the companion view to the plan. It's a month grid plus insight panes.

month grid open hours lightest days coming up needs attention recent
  • Open hours — how much free time each day actually has.
  • Lightest days — where to move things if the week is tight.
  • Coming up / needs attention / recent — focused lists across all your Tings.
  • Month grid legend — done / planned / on agenda.

Tap any day or chip to dig in. Triple-tap a day header for the hidden day agenda audit (a power-user scorecard with copy/export).

Calendar PDF import

You can import a calendar PDF (Settings → calendar import). Tings reads meetings from an Outlook/Teams agenda print or a Google Calendar schedule print and adds them as timed items already marked done on today's list.

  1. Choose PDF — Tings extracts events and shows a count ("N event(s) ready").

  2. Preview — every parsed event as a checkbox row with a live summary ("N of M selected · X timed · Y all-day"). Select all / none, deselect individually.

  3. Import meetings — only checked rows are added. Status reports what happened.

Credit habit

"Count meeting time toward" a build/limit habit (like Work). Meeting minutes merge per day (overlaps union, not double-count); the habit is auto-flipped to breakable if it isn't already.

All-day events

skip them (default) or add as tasks with a date. Dated tasks never count toward Work hours.

Re-import

Updates existing rows by a stable id — unless you renamed a local copy, in which case your edit is preserved.

Clear imported

Removes every imported meeting and strips calendar-credit logs.

For a one-off export the other way, use a task's export to calendar action to push a single item to your phone calendar as an .ics.

Prayer times

Tings computes the six daily times (Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) from your city/place and uses them as dynamic anchors for time windows — so a habit tied to "Fajr +20m" moves correctly all year.

  • Islamic prayer names — show Fajr/Dhuhr/Asr/Maghrib/Isha vs Dawn/Noon/Afternoon/Sunset/Night.
  • Calculation method — North America (ISNA) by default; only change if your mosque uses a specific method (MWL, Egyptian, Karachi, Umm al-Qura, Dubai, Moonsighting, Kuwait, Qatar, Singapore, Tehran, Turkey, Other).
  • Asr school — Shafi (standard) or Hanafi (later Asr). Affects only Asr.
  • Sample prayers — the sample-habits sheet can add Fajr–Isha demos with ready-made windows.

No faith required — the same solar anchors (sunrise, sunset, dawn) work for secular routines like "walk at sunrise."

Backup, restore & cleanup

Everything lives only on this device. Export a backup regularly — and especially before clearing browser data or switching phones. There's no cloud copy.

Export / import

  1. Settings → backup → export backup. A file tings-backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json downloads.

  2. Move it somewhere safe (cloud drive, email to yourself).

  3. On any device: Settings → backup → import backup, pick the file, confirm replace. A summary tells you exactly what will change before you confirm.

Invalid files are rejected with a clear reason and never ask you to confirm. Legacy bare-array exports are still accepted.

Cleanup / retention

Old completed tasks and extra habit history are trimmed about once a month when you open the app (you'll see a notice). Tune it under Settings → cleanup:

  • Completed tasks — delete after 2 / 3 / 7 days. Protected if still referenced (as a dynamic-time anchor, schedule-link anchor, or calendar credit habit).
  • Habit history — keep newest 12 / 30 / 60 entries per habit (or off). Always preserves plan logs; tasks aren't trimmed here.
  • Clean now — runs immediately, ignoring the monthly gate.

Pin your keystone habit (meditation, workout) and link a couple of small ones right after it in Schedule. The chain runs itself each morning.

FAQ & troubleshooting

Where is my data stored?

Only in this browser/device's local storage. Clearing site data wipes it — export a backup first.

I lost my habits — can I get them back?

Only from a backup file. Without one there's no recovery (there's no server).

Why isn't my habit on today's list?

Check: is it snoozed? Hidden by a filter? Not actually due today (look in coming up in the overview)? Outside its allowed days/window? Lower priority than the day can fit? Has a same-day schedule link whose partner isn't eligible today?

Why does the agenda look empty / sparse?

The planner needs busy times and/or places to do its best work. Without them it still lists what's due, but won't show travel or open-hour calculations. Also check your home-page settings for which categories fill open time.

A habit shows "early" — what does that mean?

It has flexibility and was pulled forward into today because its usual day is packed and today has room. If today were full it would simply stay in "coming up."

The "missed" pill appeared — what happened?

Something was suggested for today but slipped off (its window closed, or the day overflowed). Tap the pill to see today's misses and anything still open from yesterday.

Can I sync between devices?

Not automatically. Use export/import to move a snapshot between devices.

How do I start completely fresh?

Settings → start over resets settings only. To wipe habits too, clear site data (after exporting a backup).

My prayer-anchored window says "add a location"

Prayer/solar anchors need coordinates. Set your city (or give the habit a place) and the resolved time will appear.

The optimizer feels slow

Turn off smarter packing (Settings → advanced) for a faster, simpler pack. The simple packer satisfies the same hard rules; it just doesn't squeeze tight days as hard.
